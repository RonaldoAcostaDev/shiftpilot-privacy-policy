Privacy Policy

ShiftPilot

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed ShiftPilot as a shift work calendar and planner for people who work rotating, night, fixed, or irregular shifts. It includes a shift calendar with rotation patterns and several jobs, shift reminders, a shift alarm clock, clocking in and out, hours, overtime, and estimated earnings, a sleep planner and sleep log, leave and holidays, shift swaps with coworkers, exports to PDF, image, and CSV, a home screen widget, and backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. Users who prefer no advertisements can buy ShiftPilot Premium, an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play that removes them. These services never receive the schedules, hours, earnings, sleep information, or coworker names recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Jobs: name, color, icon, currency, hourly rate, and premiums for night hours, overtime, Sundays, and holidays
  • Shift types, rotation patterns, day changes, extra shifts, and day notes
  • Clock entries: clock-in and clock-out times and breaks
  • Leave (vacation, sick leave, personal days, days in lieu), yearly leave balances, and holidays
  • Shift swaps: the date, the shift, and the name of the coworker involved
  • Sleep log: the times the user went to sleep and woke up, and how they rated their sleep
  • Sleep profile: hours of sleep needed, commute, time to get ready, time to wind down, chronotype, and caffeine preference
  • Preferences: language, theme, currency, time format, first day of the week, reminder and alarm settings, alarm sound, and backup settings

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share an export or to make a backup using the features available in the application.

The sleep log and sleep plan are personal wellness tools. They are calculated on the device from the user's shifts and preferences, are not medical advice, and are never sent anywhere.

To suggest the currency, the time format, and the number and date formats, the application reads the country code of the SIM card and the mobile network, and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS), happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

Information About Coworkers

When the user records a shift swap, they enter the name of a coworker. This information is stored only on the user's device and Compose R.A Studio never receives it.

The user decides what to record and is responsible for handling it in accordance with the laws that apply to them, for example by recording only the information needed to keep track of the swap. The application helps by keeping this information on the device and by allowing it to be edited or removed at any time.

What the Application Does Not Do

ShiftPilot:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS messages
  • Does not send, sell, or share the schedules, hours, earnings, sleep information, or coworker names recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, during the first 24 hours after the application is installed, when the user clocks in or out, or while the shift alarm is ringing. They appear only occasionally, when the user completes an action such as saving a job, a shift pattern, a clock entry, a sleep entry, a leave, a holiday, or a shift swap, or exporting or backing up their data, after several actions and with a minimum time between two advertisements. When the action opens another application or the system file picker, such as sharing an export, the advertisement is shown first.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The schedules, hours, earnings, sleep information, and coworker names recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google Play Billing (ShiftPilot Premium)

ShiftPilot Premium is an optional one-time purchase whose only effect is removing all advertisements; every other feature is available without paying. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered in Google Play and are never received by the application or by Compose R.A Studio. Google handles the purchase, its receipt, and any refund according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

To know whether Premium is active, the application asks Google Play which purchases the Google account on the device owns and stores only an indicator (active or not) on the device. That indicator is not included in backups and is kept when the user deletes all the data from the application, and the purchase can be restored on another device signed in with the same Google account. While Premium is active, the application does not request or show advertisements.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Exports (PDF, image, and CSV): the monthly calendar, the hours, the shifts, and the estimated earnings can be shared through the Android share menu or saved in the location the user chooses.
  • Backups: a backup file can be saved in the location the user chooses, as described below.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share a file, the application creates a temporary copy in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From More → Data, the user can create a backup file (.shiftbackup) with all the information of the application: jobs, shift types, patterns, day changes, clock entries, leave, holidays, shift swaps, sleep log, notes, and preferences. The file is created only when the user requests it and is saved in the location the user chooses. If the user turns on automatic backup, the application saves a backup about once a week in the folder the user selects and keeps the five most recent ones. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The backup file is not encrypted and may contain the names of the user's coworkers, so it should be kept in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces the information currently stored in the application. Before restoring, the application keeps a safety copy of the current information in its private storage.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the application's database and settings in the backup of the user's Google account, or copy them when moving to a new device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Reminders, Alarm, and Notifications

When reminders are enabled, the application schedules on the device a notification before each shift and, optionally, a summary the evening before. When the shift alarm clock is enabled, it rings before each shift and shows a full-screen alarm, even when the device is locked. These notifications and the alarm screen show the shift name, its times, and the job name, and may be visible on the lock screen depending on the device's settings.

Reminders can be changed or turned off in More → Settings → Reminders, and the alarm in Rest → Alarm settings. Notifications can also be disabled from the device settings.

Home Screen Widget and Shortcuts

The optional home screen widget shows the current or next shift, its times, the job name, and the coming days, so this information is visible to anyone who can see the device's home screen. The widget is added and removed by the user. The application's shortcuts (clock in or out, log sleep, calendar, and export) only open the application and run the chosen action on the device.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. To protect it further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device and to keep backup files in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit or delete jobs, shift types, patterns, clock entries, sleep entries, leave, holidays, and shift swaps from within the application, as available on each screen
  • Delete all the information at once from More → Data → Delete all data
  • Turn off automatic backup and delete backup files from the folder where they were saved
  • Delete everything by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, exports, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.
